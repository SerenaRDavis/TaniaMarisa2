Things people wonder before they write

What kinds of things do women bring to sessions?

+

Everything. Business decisions, career pivots, relationships they're unsure about, love lives that need honest reflection, health choices they've been circling, dreams they haven't given themselves permission to start. If it's been living rent-free in your mind and body, it belongs in a session.

What actually happens in a session?

+

You begin with a short check-in so I understand what you're carrying into the space. From there, I follow the energy. Sessions are conversational, not performative. We move through it together, and you leave with something concrete to hold onto.

Do I need to prepare anything beforehand?

+

No ritual, no research required. "I've been feeling stuck and I don't know why" is more than enough to begin. Come as you are.

Is this for me if I'm new to readings?

+

Completely. There's nothing to believe in, nothing to get right, and no stage of the journey that's too early or too late. The only requirement is a genuine desire to get clear.

How long until you write back?

+

Spots open seasonally and I reach out personally, not through automated sequences. Everyone who leaves a card hears from me directly. If you have a timeframe in mind, mention it on your card.
